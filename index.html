Transfer

Concurrent fragments Parallel chunks per download. Higher is faster but heavier on your connection.
Speed limit (KB/s) A ceiling for the whole download, not per connection. Setting one turns off concurrent fragments, since each of those would get the full limit to itself. 0 means unlimited.
Use download archive Skips playlist items you have already downloaded before.
Fast trim Only affects Trim Video. Fetches just the selected range instead of the whole video. Turn off to download in full and cut afterwards — slower, but it works on sources that refuse the direct fetch.
Precise trim Only affects Trim Video. Cuts exactly on the timestamps by re-encoding the clip; off snaps to the nearest keyframe and just copies it. This is the slow one — re-encoding to webm took roughly 19× as long as mp4 in testing, so leave it off for webm unless you need the exact frame.

Video output Video mode

Container mkv holds almost any stream as-is; mp4 is the most portable but sometimes has to re-encode to fit one in.

Audio output Audio mode

Format What audio-only downloads are converted to.
